## Introduction

The one-dimensional focusing nonlinear Schrödinger equation (NLSE)

\[
i\partial_t u + \tfrac{1}{2}\partial_{xx}u + \gamma|u|^2u = 0
\]

The hypothesis tested here is not merely that this solution exists, but that a split-step Fourier method (SSFM) implementation preserves the soliton's structural integrity — quantified by symmetry-adapted orbit distance and conserved quantities — to within a prescribed error tolerance. This matters because soliton-based models underpin fiber-optic communications and matter-wave physics; numerical artifacts that mimic physical radiation or center drift can be mistaken for genuine instability.
